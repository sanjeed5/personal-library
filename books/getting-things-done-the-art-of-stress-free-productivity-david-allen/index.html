Getting Things Done: The Art of Stress-Free Productivity

by David Allen

In today's world, yesterday's methods just don't work. In Getting Things Done, veteran coach and management consultant David Allen shares the breakthrough methods for stress-free performance that he has introduced to tens of thousands of people across the country. Allen's premise is simple: our productivity is directly proportional to our ability to relax. Only when our minds are clear and our thoughts are organized can we achieve effective productivity and unleash our creative potential.

Notes

Great content, but could have been more condensed. I think the core ideas will impact me for a lifetime. Highly recommended if you have a lot of projects going on simultaneously and need a good way to organize things. But this won't be enough. You always need a system made for you, by you. GTD provides a good baseline to start though. I though of reading it after Khe Hy, Ali Abdaal recommended. But actually read it after Shreyas Doshi recommended it in a Recommendations for PMs Twitter thread.
